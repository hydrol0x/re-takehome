# m03_recip9

Let $x$ and $y$ be positive real numbers with $x + y = 1$. Prove that
$$\left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{y}\right) \ge 9,$$
with equality when $x = y = \tfrac12$.

Prove the theorem `m03_recip9`. After clearing denominators the inequality
reduces to $9xy \le (x+1)(y+1) = xy + 2$, i.e. $4xy \le 1 = (x+y)^2$, which is
$(x-y)^2 \ge 0$.
