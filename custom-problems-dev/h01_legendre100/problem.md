# h01_legendre100

Find the largest natural number $k$ such that $3^k$ divides $100!$.

Fill in `h01_answer` with a numeric literal and prove the theorem. `IsGreatest S a`
means `a ∈ S` and `a` is an upper bound of `S`; the upper-bound half needs an
argument that works for every larger `k`, not a finite check.
