# h06_order_mod125

Find the multiplicative order of $2$ modulo $125$, i.e. the least positive
integer $n$ with $2^n \equiv 1 \pmod{125}$.

Fill in `h06_answer` with a numeric literal and prove the theorem. `IsLeast S a`
means `a ∈ S` and `a` is a lower bound of `S`; the lower-bound half needs an
argument ruling out every smaller exponent, not just a finite check of a few.
