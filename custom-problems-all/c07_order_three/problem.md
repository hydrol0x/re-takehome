# c07_order_three

Find the least positive integer $n$ such that $11$ divides $3^n - 1$ (the multiplicative
order of $3$ modulo $11$).

Fill in `c07_answer` with a numeric literal and prove the theorem. `IsLeast S a`
means `a ∈ S` and `a` is a lower bound of `S`, so the proof has two parts:
your `n` works, and nothing smaller does.
