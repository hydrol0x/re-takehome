# m01_dvd13

Prove that for every natural number $n$,
$$13 \mid 4^{2n+1} + 3^{n+2}.$$

Prove the theorem `m01_dvd13`. A natural route is induction on $n$: the base
case is $4 + 9 = 13$, and each step multiplies the two powers by $16$ and $3$
respectively, so the difference of consecutive terms is again a multiple of $13$.
